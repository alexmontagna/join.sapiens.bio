1What Sapiens is and what it is used for

Sapiens is a personal relationship manager. It keeps every person who matters on one living card: their face, birthday, family ties, the city they live in and the languages they speak. It works on your own from the first day.

Sapiens is used to treat:

  • friendships that drift without anyone deciding
  • birthdays remembered one day too late
  • “we should catch up soon” that never happens
  • family nobody can place on the tree
  • people scattered across cities and time zones

2Before you use Sapiens

Do not use Sapiens if you are looking for followers, likes or strangers. It has none of them.

Sapiens contains no advertising. It does not sell or rent your data and does not train AI on it.

Tell the people you love that you use Sapiens. It works alone, and better as they join: friends who join wear their own face on your card, and you can message them one to one.

3How to use Sapiens

The recommended dose is one Moment whenever something happens: a coffee, a call, a walk. Write it in one sentence. Sapiens keeps when and where.

Each Moment restarts that person's clock, and the leaf on their card stays green.

If you forget a dose, the leaf turns brown and asks for water. Do not take a double dose. Just say hello.

If someone needs you, pin them. A high pin reminds you once on your phone, a top pin twice.

If you use more Sapiens than you should: no overdose is known.

4Possible side effects

Like all good habits, Sapiens can cause side effects, although not everybody gets them.

Very common (more than 1 in 10)
Remembering a birthday before it happens. Knowing what you talked about last time.
Common (up to 1 in 10)
Unexpected dinners. Being called back. A family tree that finally makes sense.
Uncommon (up to 1 in 100)
Knowing the weather in your cousin's city before you call.
Rare (up to 1 in 1,000)
A group chat that actually plans something.
Very rare (up to 1 in 10,000)
Meeting a future best friend through two friends who already knew you both. Sapiens suggests the introduction and asks you first.

The frequencies follow the format of a real leaflet. The side effects are hopes, not clinical data.

5How to store Sapiens

Keep within reach of the people you love.

Your data stays yours. Export everything in one click. Deleting your account really deletes it. Other members never see your contacts.
