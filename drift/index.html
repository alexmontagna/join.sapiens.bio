Sapiens is gravity

It notices the drift before you do.

Sapiens is a personal relationship manager. It works on your own from the first day, with nobody else on board.

  • The leaf turns brown

    Every Moment you log (a coffee, a call, a walk) restarts that person's clock. A card gone quiet turns its leaf brown and asks for water.

  • Your Orbit, on one map

    Everyone around you, sorted by Life Area (Family, Social, Work, Services), by city, age or language.

  • Every birthday, ahead of time

    Faces, birthdays, family ties, the city they live in and the languages they speak, on one living card. Bring them from Google in one click, or add them by hand.

  • Pins that ring

    Someone needs you this month? Pin them. A high pin reminds you once on your phone, a top pin twice.

Built for 150, not 5,000

Your mind holds about 150 people.

Anthropologist Robin Dunbar found that we keep our relationships in layers. The sky behind this page holds exactly that many lights.

When they join

Two orbits become one world.

Sapiens is invitation-only. When someone you keep joins, they wear their own face on your card, and you can talk one to one.

  • Write to exactly these people

    A post to just your family, your students, this city. Or a broadcast to a whole circle.

  • Plans with a who

    Events with a when and a where, checklists with a job per person.

  • Introductions, asked first

    When two of your people should meet, Sapiens suggests it to you. Nothing is sent until you say so.
